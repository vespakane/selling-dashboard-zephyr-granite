# Selling dashboard

A single-page, pixel-measured recreation of a mobile selling dashboard. Open `index.html` in a browser.

Tap the cart icon in the header to open a sheet where you can set the year-to-date total, active and order counts, the buyer-paid badge, total funds, and feedback counts. Values persist in the browser.

Turn on **Automate daily** in the same sheet to have the figures move on their own, around the clock. Set a monthly sales target, the price range you sell in, the band your listing count stays within, and the fee percentage. Every order gets its own timestamp on a realistic daily curve (quiet overnight, evening peak, weekends a little busier), so orders and the buyer-paid badge tick up through the day and reset at midnight, the year-to-date total climbs with each sale, the active listing count changes by one at random moments (netting at most five a day), and total funds is the net value of sales from three days earlier released over the last 24 hours. On top of that sit regimes and shocks: demand drifts week to week (strong and soft weeks), and roughly every month or two there is a demand slump (sales down 25 to 55 percent for 3 to 10 days) or a surge (up 30 to 90 percent for 2 to 6 days), a stock-out (listings fall by 60 to 200 over a few days, sales fall with them, then both recover as stock is relisted) or a restock (a batch of 50 to 150 new listings that lifts sales and sells through over a few weeks), plus the odd dead day and rare viral spike. Supply shocks can push the listing count outside its usual band for a while. The note under the automation settings says what regime is in force today. The simulation is seeded and computed from the moment you saved, so it catches up on time away and shows the same numbers for any given instant. Year-to-date and active continue from what you typed; orders and funds are fully simulated. The page refreshes itself every 15 seconds while open.

`tools/` holds the headless-Chrome render and measurement scripts used to line the layout up against reference captures (the captures themselves are not included).
